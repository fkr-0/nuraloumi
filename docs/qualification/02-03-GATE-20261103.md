Use `scripts/build-sl101-armv7.sh` **without** `--prepare` for local-only
cross qualification. The sysroot is generated under `target/sl101-cross`.
At the initial attempt all thirteen former preflight paths were present,
yet target `libxkbcommon.so` was absent and the linker failed. Loader SHA-256
at that time: `15f12fdf4f748ef845bab1cf5864379d621ef2fe94e83d65e0fc360757a3d6bc`.
The updated preparer copies the tablet-matched XKB runtime **only if an
independently authorized future prepare invocation is made**. This review
did not invoke it. The updated local preflight reports PENDING instead of
starting a predictably failing cross link.

The cross-build wrapper must inspect every Cargo-declared shipping binary,
reject missing or stray stale release executables, and defer to the ARM32
ELF/no-NEON heuristics for all those binaries. Even passing those heuristics
would **not** prove no-SIGILL or physical device acceptance.
